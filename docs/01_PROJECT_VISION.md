# EPRP Project Vision

## 1. Product name

**EPROM Progress Report (EPRP)**

## 2. Purpose

EPRP is an internal, multi-project engineering progress management platform. It gives Project Control one reliable place to collect project updates, monitor performance, preserve history, and produce management-ready reports.

## 3. The problem

Project updates are often collected through separate emails, spreadsheets, and documents. This creates duplicate entry, unclear ownership, late submissions, inconsistent terminology, and significant manual work when preparing Monthly or Executive Reports.

## 4. The solution

EPRP connects:

```text
Projects & master data
        ↓
Department updates
        ↓
Weekly Reports
        ↓
Monthly compilation
        ↓
Executive / company-president report
```

The same approved information should flow through the system without repeated copy/paste.

## 5. Users

- **System Administrator:** manages users, master data, configuration, and access.
- **Project Control Admin:** creates reporting cycles, collects updates, reviews data, and issues reports.
- **Project Manager:** reviews the project and its department updates.
- **Department Lead/User:** edits only assigned project and department information.
- **Reviewer/Approver:** reviews, returns, approves, or finalizes reports.
- **Executive/Company President:** reads portfolio and project Executive Reports.
- **External Contributor:** optional restricted contributor using a secure link or Excel exchange.

## 6. Main modules

- Portfolio Dashboard
- Projects and Project Setup
- Departments, Systems, Disciplines, and Contacts
- Organization Chart
- Weekly Reports
- Monthly Reports
- Executive Reports
- Risks, Issues, and Actions
- Collaboration and Comment Register
- Documents and Attachments
- Administration and Permissions

## 7. Product principles

1. **Project-centric:** all operational information belongs to a project.
2. **Single source of truth:** the platform stores the approved data; files are exchange or output formats.
3. **Progressive detail:** executives see summaries; project teams see the details they need.
4. **Traceability:** preserve who changed what, when, and why.
5. **Controlled flexibility:** master data is editable by authorized administrators, not hardcoded in the UI.
6. **Reuse:** Weekly and Monthly use shared components and concepts.
7. **Professional output:** reports must be suitable for management review and A4/PDF distribution.

## 8. MVP scope

The first useful release should support:

- Multiple projects and project master data.
- Project-specific departments, systems, disciplines, contacts, and responsibilities.
- Weekly Workspace with project information, KPIs, activities, department updates, risks, issues, Next Week Plan, Look Ahead, comments, attachments, and workflow status.
- Department-scoped submissions.
- Monthly Draft compiled from approved Weeklies, plus new Monthly comments.
- Portfolio and project Executive views.
- Professional A4/PDF output.
- Login and role-based access when the existing database foundation is ready.

## 9. Deferred items

These should be added only after the core reporting flow works:

- Advanced email automation and reminders.
- Excel import for external contributors.
- DOCX generation.
- QR codes, signatures, and company branding administration.
- Primavera, Power BI, AI summaries, and other integrations.
